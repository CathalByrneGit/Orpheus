The machine proposes; you decide. A survey is good at noticing that the same kind of thing keeps appearing and that it keeps carrying the same handful of attributes. It is bad at what follows — whether two of these are one type with a role, whether a property should have been a type of its own. Those are the commitments that make an ontology yours, and accepting an object type fixes the shape of every row that will ever be filed under it.
